Understanding Artificial Intelligence

Module Overview

This module explored the foundations of artificial intelligence, including machine learning techniques, ethical considerations and practical business applications. Through collaborative discussions and individual assessments, I examined AI adoption and evaluated machine learning models for financial services use cases.

Learning Outcomes

The module is structured around the following four learning outcomes.

  1. Understand the legal, ethical and professional issues brought up by AI and the impact of AI on society.
  2. Understand and critically analyse the essential concepts, principles, methods, techniques and problems of AI.
  3. Demonstrate a critical understanding of data requirements and programming paradigms applicable to AI.
  4. Apply and evaluate critically the various methods, tools and technologies applied to an AI project in order to develop an effective plan and delivery of solutions to a business problem.

Reflective Learning

Module Reflection

This module strengthened my understanding of artificial intelligence, moving beyond its theoretical foundations to examine how machine learning can address practical business challenges. In particular, the individual assessments provided an opportunity to explore AI applications within financial services, building on my professional experience in banking technology and core banking transformation.

The AI Solution Implementation assessment was particularly valuable in developing my understanding of model evaluation and the trade-offs involved in selecting machine learning algorithms. Comparing different approaches to credit risk assessment reinforced the importance of considering not only predictive performance, but also explainability, fairness and the wider regulatory environment.

Reflecting on the module, I recognise that successful AI adoption requires more than technical capability. Effective implementation depends on robust governance, appropriate data management and alignment with business objectives. These insights are directly relevant to my interest in applying emerging AI capabilities to banking modernisation, risk management and operational efficiency.
