Bernard (Ben) Westlake

Delivery Lead Manager, Accenture UK

Bernard (Ben) Westlake brings over eight years of experience in core banking technology and transformation, specialising in banking products, solution design and implementation. He is currently a Delivery Lead Manager at Accenture UK, supporting core banking programmes and working with business and technology stakeholders to deliver complex change.

His work has included Temenos Transact implementations, upgrades and post-go-live support, covering deposits, lending, accounts and financial accounting. He has also led business analysis teams and contributed to product design, data migration and testing. Bernard holds Temenos Transact accreditation and is Mambu certified.

Having started his career in retail banking and debt collections, he brings first-hand knowledge of banking operations to technology delivery, alongside his consulting and platform experience.

Academic Background

Bernard holds a BA in English and Geography (Joint Major) from University College Dublin, an MPhil in Popular Literature from Trinity College Dublin, and a Higher Diploma in Science (FinTech) from the National College of Ireland.

He is currently undertaking an MSc in Artificial Intelligence through the University of Essex Online. The programme builds on his financial technology experience, with a particular interest in machine learning, model explainability and the responsible application of AI in banking.

Professional Interests

Bernard's interests sit at the intersection of core banking technology, financial services and artificial intelligence. He is particularly interested in how banks can modernise existing platforms while adopting AI in ways that are effective, explainable and well governed.
