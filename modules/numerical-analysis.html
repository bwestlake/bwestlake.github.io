Numerical Analysis

Module Overview

This module introduces the mathematical and statistical principles underpinning data science and artificial intelligence. Through practical exercises using R, I am developing my understanding of data analysis, statistical techniques and how to interpret results. Building on my previous exposure to statistics, the module also provides an opportunity to refresh my knowledge and strengthen my ability to evaluate data, draw meaningful conclusions and apply statistical methods to support decision-making.

Module focus

Statistical Analysis with R

Exploring data, presenting results and applying statistical techniques through practical programming exercises.

R programming · Data visualisation · Statistical methods

Learning Outcomes

This module is structured around four learning outcomes, covering the mathematical and statistical foundations of data science and artificial intelligence, the practical application of analytical techniques, and the interpretation, critical evaluation and communication of statistical results to support informed decision-making.

  1. Demonstrate systematic understanding of the key mathematical and statistical concepts and techniques which underpin mechanisms in Data Science and AI.
  2. EApply mathematical and statistical methods in these fields to help in the decision-making process.
  3. Critically evaluate the use of statistical analysis and the numeric interpretation of results as aids in the decision-making process.
  4. Critically appraise and present results of a statistical analysis to a diverse audience.

Formative Assessment 01

Unit 1

Types and Sources of Data and an Introduction to R: This activity introduces exploratory data analysis in R, examining the structure and characteristics of a COVID-19 dataset covering Indian States and Union Territories. R commands are used to identify variable types, count observations, determine the reporting period and identify the states with the highest reporting frequency.

Data Structures in R: This activity explores the use of frequency tables, percentage calculations and binary variables in R to analyse COVID-19 recovery reporting patterns. The analysis distinguishes between daily state-level reports with and without recorded recoveries, using labelled categories to improve the interpretation and presentation of results.
