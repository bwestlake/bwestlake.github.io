Overview

This matrix provides an overview of the technical, academic and professional skills I am developing throughout the MSc Artificial Intelligence programme. It draws on my assessments, practical work and experience in banking technology.

The proficiency levels are self-assessed and reflect where I currently stand. While some skills are well established through my professional experience, others are still developing. I intend to revisit the matrix as I progress through the programme.

Development Priorities

My professional experience has given me a strong foundation in banking technology and delivery. The MSc is helping me build on that experience by developing a better understanding of artificial intelligence and its practical applications.

The programme has also highlighted some areas where I need to develop further, particularly my practical programming and statistical analysis skills. While I have gained experience using WEKA, I would like to become more confident working independently with Python and R.

As I progress through the programme, I intend to revisit this matrix and update my proficiency levels to reflect new knowledge, practical experience and completed assessments.
